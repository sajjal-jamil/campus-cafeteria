How to Place an Order

Follow these quick and easy steps to grab your meals without any hassle.

1

Browse & Choose

Check out our menu or daily deals board to pick your favorite items and combos.

2

Pay at Counter

Head over to the billing counter, state your order, and pay securely via cash or student card.

3

Collect Token

Receive your printed order token and keep it safe while you relax or chat with friends.

4

Pick Up Meal

Listen for your token number announcement at the pickup counter and collect your fresh meal!

Cafeteria Guidelines & Pick-Up Rules

  • Peak Hours Notice: Expect higher wait times between 1:00 PM and 2:30 PM due to lunch rush. We recommend pre-ordering or visiting slightly earlier.
  • Tray Return Policy: Please clear your table and return trays and empty cups to the designated return stations after dining.
  • Dietary Inquiries: Feel free to ask the counter staff regarding ingredients if you have severe food allergies or specific dietary requirements.
  • Seating Etiquette: Please keep tables tidy and share seating spaces cordially during rush hours so fellow students can find a spot.
